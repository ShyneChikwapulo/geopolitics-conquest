# Privacy Policy

Geopolitics Conquest is a demo application built as an entrance exam
submission for INF4001N at the University of Cape Town. It is not a
commercial product.

## What we collect
- **Email and password** if you sign up (via Firebase Authentication).
  Passwords are hashed by Firebase; we never see them.
- **Game state** for games you play (via Firebase Firestore): countries,
  soldiers, turn order, action log. This is what makes games shareable and
  replayable.
- **Nothing else.** No analytics tracking, no cookies beyond Firebase's
  own session cookie, no third-party pixels.

## What we do with it
- Store it in Google Firebase so that (a) you can resume a game on another
  device, and (b) anyone with the game link (`?watch=<id>`) can view the
  game read-only.
- Nothing else. No advertising, no selling, no sharing.

## Your rights
- You can delete your account and all associated games at any time by
  emailing the maintainer or by using Firebase's user management console.
- Data is retained until the account is deleted.
